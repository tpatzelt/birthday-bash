## Notes

- `idle` never touches the screen. It is expected to fail everywhere except
  L4, where doing nothing is still the safest available play — though after
  the difficulty pass it is no longer close to a guarantee.
- `mash` is the no-crash canary; its win rate carries no meaning.
- "tipsy on eased" is the mercy rule doing its job: after four fails the
  level auto-eases and must then be winnable by the worst bot we model.
- The four medians land under the 5–7 minute target from DESIGN.md §2 once
  intro cards, fails and the reveal are added. That is the deliberate side to
  err on at a party: a game that ends too soon still reaches the present, and
  PLAN.md §4 spends the remaining time on finishing rather than lengthening.
- The difficulty pass raised every goal (26 Flaschen, 3000 px queue, 28
  Heringe, 5400 px river) and, where the bots had room, the rate the level
  comes at you. What bounded each level was not taste but a gate:
  - **L1** — `tipsy`. Obstacle share and spawn gap are savage to it: 0.46
    share took tipsy to 48 %, and tightening the gap to 185–300 px to 15 %.
    Length and the speed ramp cost it far less, so the goal carries the pass.
  - **L2** — `perfect`, and it is a design signal, not a bot weakness: a
    two-bouncer row can close its gap, and the Sonnenbrille is what carries a
    perfect line through. Shortening `shadesDurationMs` dropped perfect to
    95 %. Hence a longer queue and a modest `twoBouncerChance`, nothing more.
  - **L3** — `tipsy` again; 32 Heringe put it on the 50 % line, 28 leaves air.
  - **L4** — `idle`. Narrowing the channel or thickening the rock field guts
    it (10 % and 8 %), and doing nothing must stay the safest play, not a
    trap — the joke in DESIGN.md §4 depends on it. A longer, slightly
    narrower river keeps idle at a comfortable margin over its 35 % floor.
