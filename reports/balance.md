## Notes

- `idle` never touches the screen. It is expected to fail everywhere except
  L4, where doing nothing is the correct play — that is the joke.
- `mash` is the no-crash canary; its win rate carries no meaning.
- "tipsy on eased" is the mercy rule doing its job: after four fails the
  level auto-eases and must then be winnable by the worst bot we model.
- The four medians land under the 5–7 minute target from DESIGN.md §2 once
  intro cards, fails and the reveal are added. That is the deliberate side to
  err on at a party: a game that ends too soon still reaches the present, and
  PLAN.md §4 spends the remaining time on finishing rather than lengthening.
