## Notes

- `idle` never touches the screen and now loses everywhere, L4 included.
  Doing nothing is still the *safest* play there — it survives several times
  longer than `mash` — but the narrower channel means it no longer reaches
  the Spree on its own. That relationship, not a win rate, is what
  tests/core/bots.test.ts pins down.
- The `perfect` column is a count on purpose. `perfect` is greedy, not an
  oracle — it scores the next moment, not the next ten — so on the tuned L1
  and L2 it corners itself on about one seed in two hundred. Those seeds are
  fair: they pass the no-unavoidable-death lookahead in fuzz.test.ts at every
  sampled frame. Hence a 97 % gate and a count here, where 199/200 cannot
  round itself into looking like 200/200.
- `mash` is the no-crash canary; its win rate carries no meaning.
- "tipsy on eased" is the mercy rule doing its job: after four fails the
  level auto-eases and must then be winnable by the worst bot we model.
- The medians sit inside the 5–7 minute target from DESIGN.md §2 once intro
  cards, fails and the reveal are added — and the second pass moved them the
  right way: the levels now take long enough to be worth clearing.
- **Second difficulty pass.** The first pass was bounded by the old gates
  (casual ≥ 85 %, tipsy ≥ 50 %), and the result played too easy: nothing but
  L4 could realistically be lost. Those two gates were the wrong instrument —
  what has to hold is *he reaches the reveal*, and that is guaranteed by the
  mercy rules (skip after 2 fails, auto-ease after 4), not by a level nobody
  can lose. So the un-eased `tipsy` floor is gone, the `casual` floor dropped
  to 55 %, and the mercy ease was strengthened to carry the promise instead
  (density ×0.65, speed ×0.82, +2 lives — was ×0.8 / ×0.9 / +1).
  Goals: 38 Flaschen, 4300 px queue, 36 Heringe, 6400 px river. Faster
  everywhere, denser everywhere, and a smaller Tüte in L3.
  What bounds each level now:
  - **L1** — the speed ramp (6.0 px/s², cap 330) is what makes the back half
    hurt, and **two lives instead of three** is what stops a good run from
    being unloseable: it moved `casual` from 97 % to 88 % on its own.
  - **L2** — the `perfect` bot. A two-bouncer row can close its own gap, so
    `bouncerSpeedMax` is capped at 130: at 150 the fuzz test found a row
    configuration no input escaped, which is a level bug, not difficulty.
    Below that cap the level is fair but not gentle.
  - **L3** — `perfect` at the spawn rate: 0.50 s → 0.26 s over 32 s, with a
    56 px Tüte and a third of everything falling being vegetables.
  - **L4** — the Ruhe economy. A narrower channel (80 → 55 px half-width)
    over a longer river is what did the work; the rock field was left thin
    on purpose, because rocks punish the panicking player twice.
